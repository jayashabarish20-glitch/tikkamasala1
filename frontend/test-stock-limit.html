Stock Limit Validation Test

Test Setup

This test validates that cart quantities never exceed available stock.

TEST 1: Normal Increment Within Stock

Stock = 65, Quantity = 1 → Click "+" → Should become 2

TEST 2: Increment at Near Limit

Stock = 65, Quantity = 64 → Click "+" → Should become 65

TEST 3: Prevent Increment at Limit

Stock = 65, Quantity = 65 → Click "+" → Should remain 65

TEST 4: Rapid Clicks at Limit

Stock = 65, Quantity = 65 → Click "+" rapidly 5 times → Should remain 65

TEST 5: Rapid Clicks Near Limit

Stock = 10, Quantity = 9 → Click "+" 5 times rapidly → Should become 10 (not 11+)
